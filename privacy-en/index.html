Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

简体中文   /   English

This policy explains how the Swinglo iPhone app and this website handle information. Swinglo is developed by yuhongda. For questions, contact silverage.y@gmail.com.

Apple Music and your media library

Swinglo requests access to Apple Music and your media library when it needs to read music. With your permission, the app uses Apple's MusicKit to read playlists and track information you choose, check playback eligibility, and play music. Swinglo does not modify your Apple Music library. You can revoke access in iOS Settings, although related features may no longer work. Apple's handling of its music services is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Data on your device

Your playlist choices, playback preferences, theme, and other settings are stored on your device. To display and control playback, the widget and app share the current track, artwork, and related state through an on-device shared container. Public playlist presets are cached locally for use when the network is unavailable. Swinglo does not upload your custom playlist choices to the developer's server.

Network and third-party services

Swinglo reads developer-published playlist presets from Apple's public CloudKit database and uses Apple Music to obtain playlists, tracks, and playback content. Artwork may also be loaded from its source URL. These requests require a network connection, and service providers may process connection information under their own policies. Swinglo does not write your personal playlist choices to the public CloudKit database or store audio there. Apple services are subject to Apple's own privacy policy.

Website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection and access data under its policies; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website does not use analytics or advertising tools.

Retention and deletion

Local settings and caches remain on your device until you delete the app or the system clears them. Deleting the app generally removes its local data; system backups and data in Apple Music are managed by iOS and Apple services. You can manage music permissions in Settings and your library in Apple Music.

Children's privacy

Swinglo does not require a developer account and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. If you believe we have received a child's personal information through Swinglo, please contact us.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the product or services change and will publish the updated date on this page. For privacy questions, contact silverage.y@gmail.com.
